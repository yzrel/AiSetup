SUMMARYOFASSESSMENT
BACKGROUND:
TSM Food Products was established in 2015 and was originally located at Purok 4B, Balindog, Kidapawan City. The owner, Ms. Elizabeth V. Moneva, started the firm with four employees. At the onset of the business, the capitalization was ₱25,000, and the initial products were calamansi ready-to-drink juice and concentrate.
METHODOLOGY
The assessment was conducted through a comprehensive approach that included on-site plant visits, direct observation of the workflow and facilities, interviews with the owner, Ms. Elizabeth V. Moneva, along with key production staff, and a thorough review of the enterprise’s financial, marketing, and operational documents.
SUMMARYOFFINDINGS
1. Strategic Direction 
Mission Statement: 
To produce high-quality, safe, and affordable calamansi-based beverages and related products that promote the health and wellness of consumers while supporting sustainable livelihood and economic development in the community.
Vision Statement: 
To be a leading producer of innovative and world-class calamansi products in Mindanao, recognized for excellence in quality, sustainability, and social responsibility by 2030.
Plans
The company shall pursue continuous improvement in product quality, food safety compliance, and production efficiency through investment in modern equipment, workforce training, and research and development. 
Strategic goals include:
 (1) full compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and food safety standards;
(2) expansion of product lines;
(3) increased annual production capacity;
(4) adoption of sustainable waste management systems; and 
(5) strengthened market presence through branding and promotional programs.

Work Environment

Product and Process Performance and Improvement
TSM Food Products is a food processing firm with its juice reaching up to 1,570,000 bottles per year. Listed in the table below are the corresponding annual production volume per finished good.
Presently, recipes are strictly followed wherein ingredients are weighedusing a digital scale. Forms used are output per day, inventory monitoring, work instruction, purchase order, and daily time record. Parameter set is pH level, and quality checking is administered before, within, and after production. Also, inspection upon receipt is done and recorded.
On the other hand, packing and labelling is done manually and appropriate workstations are not designated. Yield analysis is not recorded. Similarly, documented quality control procedures and standards are not in place.
Furthermore, product and raw material usage standards are available in file. Returns (3%) are encountered due to bad orders and are not recorded. Nobody is formally in chargein promoting quality consciousness among workers.
Environmental Management System
Waste Management
Segregation practiced; hazardous wastes stored for accredited disposal; calamansi peelings composted; covered drainage present. No three-chamber wastewater treatment tank yet; attachments include technical guidance for a three-chamber system and utility sinks.
Methods of disposal
Collected by the City’s garbage collector in a scheduled manner. Calamansi peelings are disposed ofthrough composting. Also, facility’s drainage system is covered. However, a three-chamber tank for wastewater treatment is not provided.
OTHER OBSERVATIONS: 
Ventilation is insufficient; industrial exhaust fans and roofing insulation are recommended.
Materials handling: push carts and trolley available; additional racks, plastic pallets, and bottle crates recommended.
CONCLUSIONS:
The facility complies with basic sanitation and safety requirements, using stainless steel tables, requiring PPE, conducting daily cleaning, and maintaining pest-free production areas. Equipment is functional, QC parameters are monitored, and production records are in place. However, several gaps exist, including absence of chemical storage cabinets, inadequate ventilation, lack of emergency lighting and first aid kits, open-type changing rooms, and insufficient documentation for QA and yield monitoring. Improvements in inventory management, occupational safety, facility layout, and wastewater treatment are also required.
With DepEd as its major market, TSM Food Products has secured a strong foothold not only in Region XII but also in the neighboring Regions XI and XIII. This strategic client base offers both stability and the potential for expansion into wider institutional markets.
RECOMMENDATIONS:
Adoption of the Tubig Talino technology from DOST-FNRI is highly recommended as it provides TSM Food Products with a unique competitive advantage. By offering fortified beverages, the company can tap into government procurement programs, particularly with DepEd, while expanding its reach to other agencies seeking nutritious and affordable products.
Strengthen quality assurance by issuing product standards, QC process flows, and work instructions, and by acquiring laboratory instruments (pH meter, refractometer, thermometer).
Implement Production Planning and Control to improve efficiency and avoid delays
Construct a three-chamber wastewater treatment system to comply with environmental standards.
